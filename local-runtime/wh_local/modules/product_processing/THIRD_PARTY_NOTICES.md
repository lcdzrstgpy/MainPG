## Distribution boundary

Repositories or search results without an explicit open-source license are not copied, bundled, or redistributed. They may have informed research-direction validation only and are intentionally absent from the runtime source manifest.

External material contributes content direction only. MainPG's existing hard constraints always take precedence over every external reference, including product-fact fidelity, title language and length rules, sensitive-word controls, exact image-grid requirements, source-image fidelity, OCR repair, image splitting, detail-image composition, publication, and fallback behavior. No external platform limits, dimensions, certifications, reviews, sales claims, discounts, guarantees, or comparative advertising claims are adopted as runtime rules.

For MIT entries, the runtime uses reviewed structural adaptations under the upstream MIT license at the pinned commit. For the CC0-1.0 entry, the runtime uses normalized content elements under the upstream CC0-1.0 dedication. Repository names and links identify provenance and do not imply endorsement.
